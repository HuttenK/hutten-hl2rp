Changes: concise original Russian summaries, roleplay procedures adapted to this server, new layouts, and newly AI-generated illustrations. These are not copies of photographs or screenshots from the source articles. SCP-3114 uses the requested Secret Laboratory profile. The department operating instructions are original server-specific writing inspired by the department roles, not a translation of the linked hub.

All seven `materials/foundation/scp*.png` assets were generated and then separately edited with the built-in image generation tool into degraded VHS archive stills: soft detail, noise, horizontal scanlines, tracking defects, muted colour and chromatic bleed. Department artwork was left clean. The SCP-173 illustration uses an original sculpture design; the former Izumi Kato photograph is not included. SCP-096 is pictured from behind with its face excluded.

Generated department illustrations in this pack may also be reused under CC BY-SA 3.0, to the extent any rights subsist. No third-party image files were downloaded. Generation and edit prompts are retained in the project's `artwork/terminal-catalog-prompts.json` and `artwork/terminal-vhs-prompts.json`.
